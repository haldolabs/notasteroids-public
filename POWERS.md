# Powers

Nineteen of them, bought with ROX from the E-SHOP. This page says what each one does and
what you will see when it does it.

**Buy as many as you like. You can equip four at once.** Seven of the nineteen answer a key;
the other twelve just work. The shop marks every row **KEYED** or **ALWAYS ON**, and with four
already in the bar it marks a row **BAR FULL**: you can still buy it, and it waits in your
inventory until you unequip something.

All four show in one bar along the bottom of the screen — abilities with their key and a
cooldown ring, always-on ones with whatever they are doing:

<p align="center"><img src="media/powers/bar.png" alt="The bar: REGOLITH ARMOR ready, CRYO-VENT on 5, ACCRETION DISK at 0 of 5, TIDAL LOCK on 6" width="560"></p>

The digits only ever land on things you can press. Equip a passive first and it takes a box
without taking a key, so `5` still fires the first real ability. Above each box, an ability
shows its key and a passive shows what it is doing — `READY` or a countdown for REGOLITH
ARMOR's shield, `0/5` for ACCRETION DISK's debris, `+15%` for HEAVY METAL's mass, `HIDDEN`
or `SPOTTED` for VANTABLACK CARBON.

## The seven that answer a key

They take <kbd>5</kbd> <kbd>6</kbd> <kbd>7</kbd> <kbd>8</kbd>, in the order you have them,
with the cooldown drawn as a ring around the icon. On a controller, <kbd>X</kbd> fires the
next ready one and moves along.

| | | ROX | Cooldown | What it does | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="media/powers/icons/cryo-vent.png" width="40" alt=""> | **CRYO-VENT** | 400 | 8s | A shotgun of ice shards. Short range, heavy damage | The shards |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/lodestone-pull.png" width="40" alt=""> | **LODESTONE PULL** | 300 | 12s | Hauls you at the nearest AXIOM ship, hard | You lurch |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/slingshot-maneuver.png" width="40" alt=""> | **SLINGSHOT MANEUVER** | 350 | 15s | Throws you sideways past the nearest coloured ship, fast, and turns you to face the new heading. It needs a coloured ship on screen; with none, it says so and stays ready | You move — hard |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/polarity-shift.png" width="40" alt=""> | **POLARITY SHIFT** | 350 | 18s | Shoves every ship near you away, and their shots with them | The ships scatter |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/tidal-lock.png" width="40" alt=""> | **TIDAL LOCK** | 400 | 20s, lasts 5s | Ships close to you are forced to face away, and slowed | **A blue ring pulsing around you** — its edge is exactly how far the effect reaches |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/thermal-expansion.png" width="40" alt=""> | **THERMAL EXPANSION** | 450 | 25s, lasts 4s | **Your rock doubles in size** and touching anything wrecks it | The rock growing, wrapped in **an orange glow** |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/binary-system.png" width="40" alt=""> | **BINARY SYSTEM** | 450 | 30s | A moon appears and orbits you, blocking shots | **The moon**, until something kills it |

**Every one of them, when it fires**, puts a coloured ring on your hull for a moment, throws
a banner across the middle of the screen with its name, and makes a sound. If you press the
key and get a grey banner instead, it tells you why: still on cooldown, or nothing to swing
off.

## The twelve that just work

Nothing to press.

| | | ROX | What it does | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="media/powers/icons/roche-limit.png" width="40" alt=""> | **ROCHE LIMIT** | 500 | Any plain ship that touches you comes apart. The coloured ones and the veterans are immune | **A purple dashed ring** around you — its edge is exactly the kill radius |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/regolith-armor.png" width="40" alt=""> | **REGOLITH ARMOR** | 350 | Absorbs the first shot that hits you, once every 10s | **Brown dust orbiting you** while it is charged, and `READY` or a countdown over the box |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/accretion-disk.png" width="40" alt=""> | **ACCRETION DISK** | 400 | Every ship you smash adds a piece of debris. At five it flings them all outward as shots and starts over | The debris count over the box, green when it is about to fire |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/heavy-metal-enrichment.png" width="40" alt=""> | **HEAVY METAL ENRICHMENT** | 500 | +5% mass per veteran you fell. Mass is what resists a shove | Your mass bonus over the box |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/sublimation-trail.png" width="40" alt=""> | **SUBLIMATION TRAIL** | 300 | Leaves gas that makes their ships turn worse | **A pale blue trail** behind you, fading, and `VENTING` over the box while you thrust |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/kessler-syndrome.png" width="40" alt=""> | **KESSLER SYNDROME** | 450 | Ships you smash leave wreckage that hurts the rest of the AXIOM | **Drifting debris** where you smashed them |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/rubble-pile.png" width="40" alt=""> | **RUBBLE PILE** | 400 | When you would be lost, you scatter into pieces instead. Gather three of five in time and you reform | **Your rock coming apart, and the pieces you have to fly back into**, with the clock over the field |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/propulsion-upgrade.png" width="40" alt=""> | **PROPULSION UPGRADE** | 150 each | Raises how far your throttle keys wind, up to three times | The thrust and spin numbers |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/iridium-core.png" width="40" alt=""> | **IRIDIUM CORE** | 300 | You plough through their ships instead of bouncing off, and a hit no longer shoves you | **A silver pulse on the hull** each time a hit would have shoved you and did not |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/vantablack-carbon.png" width="40" alt=""> | **VANTABLACK CARBON** | 250 | Their ships notice you at half the distance | `HIDDEN` over the box, and `SPOTTED` while a ship has you in range |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/elastic-collision.png" width="40" alt=""> | **ELASTIC COLLISION** | 200 | Every bounce adds 10% speed | **A pink pulse on the hull** with each bounce that added speed |
| <img src="media/powers/icons/lorentz-force.png" width="40" alt=""> | **LORENTZ FORCE** | 350 | Deflects their lasers when you are moving fast | `TOO SLOW` over the box until you are moving fast enough, then `DEFLECTING`, and **a yellow pulse on the hull** for each laser it turns |
