# Powers

Nineteen of them, bought with ROX from the E-SHOP. This page says what each actually does
and what you should expect to see, because the shop row is one line and that has not been
enough.

**You can equip three at once.** Seven of the nineteen answer a key; the other twelve just
work. The shop marks every row **KEYED** or **ALWAYS ON** before you spend anything.

---

## The seven that answer a key

They sit in the hotbar along the bottom of the screen, on <kbd>5</kbd> <kbd>6</kbd>
<kbd>7</kbd>, with the cooldown drawn as a ring. On a controller, <kbd>X</kbd> fires the
next ready one and moves along.

| | ROX | Cooldown | What it does | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CRYO-VENT** | 400 | 8s | A shotgun of ice shards. Short range, heavy damage | The shards |
| **LODESTONE PULL** | 300 | 12s | Drags nearby rocks toward you | The rocks move |
| **SLINGSHOT MANEUVER** | 350 | 15s | Throws you sideways past the nearest coloured enemy, fast, and turns you to face the new heading | You move — hard |
| **POLARITY SHIFT** | 350 | 18s | Shoves every nearby rock away from you | Everything scatters |
| **TIDAL LOCK** | 400 | 20s, lasts 5s | Enemies close to you are forced to face away, and slowed | **A blue ring pulsing around you** — its edge is exactly how far the effect reaches |
| **THERMAL EXPANSION** | 450 | 25s, lasts 4s | **Your rock doubles in size** and touching anything wrecks it | The rock growing, wrapped in **an orange glow** |
| **BINARY SYSTEM** | 450 | 30s | A moon appears and orbits you, blocking shots | **The moon**, until something kills it |

**Every one of them, when it fires**, puts a coloured ring on your hull for a moment, throws
a banner across the middle of the screen with its name, and makes a sound. If you press the
key and get none of that, it was still on cooldown.

⚠ **SLINGSHOT MANEUVER needs a coloured enemy on screen.** With none, it fires, spends its
cooldown, and does nothing at all. That is a bug, it is written down, and it will be fixed.

## The twelve that just work

Nothing to press. Some of them show you something; several are completely silent, which is
noted honestly below.

| | ROX | What it does | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ROCHE LIMIT** | 500 | Small drones that touch you disintegrate | **A purple dashed ring** around you — its edge is exactly the kill radius |
| **REGOLITH ARMOR** | 350 | Absorbs the first shot that hits you, once every 10s | **Brown dust orbiting you** while it is charged, and the HUD says ready or recharging |
| **ACCRETION DISK** | 400 | Collects debris, then launches it as shots | A debris count in the HUD, green when it is ready |
| **HEAVY METAL ENRICHMENT** | 500 | +5% mass per veteran you fell. Mass is what resists a shove | Your mass bonus in the HUD |
| **SUBLIMATION TRAIL** | 300 | Leaves gas that makes enemies turn worse | **A pale blue trail** behind you, fading |
| **KESSLER SYNDROME** | 450 | Rocks you smash leave wreckage that hurts the AXIOM | **Drifting debris** where you smashed things |
| **RUBBLE PILE** | 400 | When you die you scatter into pieces instead. Gather three of five in time and you reform | **Your rock coming apart, and the pieces you have to fly back into** |
| **PROPULSION UPGRADE** | 150 each | Raises how far your throttle keys wind, up to three times | The thrust and spin numbers |
| **IRIDIUM CORE** | 300 | You plough through enemies instead of bouncing off | Nothing — you notice by not bouncing |
| **VANTABLACK CARBON** | 250 | Enemies notice you at half the distance | Nothing |
| **ELASTIC COLLISION** | 200 | Every bounce adds 10% speed | Nothing |
| **LORENTZ FORCE** | 350 | Deflects lasers when you are moving fast | Nothing |

⚠ **The last four tell you nothing.** They are working — they change how the game behaves —
but there is no indicator, so you cannot confirm it from the screen. That is a gap we know
about.

## Why the HUD has two of these

Two corners, on purpose, answering different questions:

* **The hotbar** — the keyed abilities, their key, and whether they are ready. What you can
  press *now*.
* **The always-on readout** — the passives you are carrying. If nothing you have equipped
  takes a key it says **ALL EQUIPPED POWER-UPS ARE ALWAYS ON**, so an empty hotbar is not
  mistaken for something broken.

---

## The part that is genuinely awkward

**There are seven keyed abilities and three slots.** Buy a fourth and it sits in your
inventory doing nothing until you unequip something, and the shop does not warn you at the
counter.

Whether that stays at three is a design question — three forces you to choose, and the
keyboard has room for more. If you have an opinion, press <kbd>B</kbd> and say so. That is
exactly the kind of thing worth hearing before it is settled.
